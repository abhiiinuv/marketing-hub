Specs and tickets are the structured artifacts inside an Epic.

## Specs

Specs capture the intent behind the work. They should explain what the project needs, why it matters, and which constraints agents must preserve.

Useful spec types:

- product requirements
- technical design
- API contracts
- migration plans
- review notes
- decision logs

Specs should stay focused. Prefer several small specs over one document that tries to contain everything.

## Tickets

Tickets turn specs into executable implementation slices.

A good ticket includes:

- the goal
- relevant files or areas
- acceptance criteria
- constraints
- test expectations
- known risks

## How They Work Together

Specs hold the durable intent. Tickets drive the concrete work. Agents can use both during implementation, review, and verification.
